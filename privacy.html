Privacy policy

Updated November 2, 2026

This policy describes the current Seattle At Home website and its planned Vercel hosting.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes information handled through the Seattle At Home website operated by Emily Hawkins, a real estate broker affiliated with Coldwell Banker Danforth, Bellevue office. Separate brokerage, transaction, and third-party notices may apply when you use their services.

Information you provide

If you email, call, or book a meeting, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, availability, and information about your real estate goals. Please share only what is needed for your inquiry. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank details, identification documents, or other sensitive records through general website inquiries.

Booking takes place on Calendly. Calendly collects the details and answers you submit there and makes booking information available to Emily according to the event's settings. The current website's other inquiry forms are labeled preview-only and do not send or save submissions. That behavior and this policy must be updated before those forms are connected.

Technical information and external resources

When you visit a website, its hosting provider and resource providers may receive information such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, request time, and referring page. Production hosting is planned for Vercel; its processing depends on the services and settings enabled at launch.

  • Fonts: the site requests Manrope from Google Fonts. Loading these resources sends a request to Google.
  • Maps: the Client Stories map loads Leaflet and MapLibre resources from the unpkg content delivery network and vector map tiles from OpenFreeMap using OpenStreetMap data. Those providers receive requests, including your IP address, when the map loads. The map shows property locations; it does not request your device's precise location.
  • Videos and social profiles: the current curated videos play from site-hosted files. Instagram and YouTube profile links take you to those services; they are not automatic social feeds.

The current site code does not include advertising pixels or an analytics tracking script. This is not a statement about cookies or logging used by third-party sites, hosting infrastructure, or services enabled in the future.

Quizzes and browser storage

The current quizzes process answers in your browser to display a result. They do not send your answers to Emily or a lead service. Content Studio and Quiz Studio store drafts in your browser's local storage so they can be reopened on the same browser and device. These drafts are not published or synchronized automatically.

You can remove local drafts through your browser's site-data settings. Clearing site data may permanently remove unsaved or unexported drafts. Shared-device users should avoid saving personal information in drafts. Browser storage controls do not delete information already submitted to Calendly or sent by email.

Newsletter signup

The homepage newsletter form is separate from preview-only inquiry forms. Once its server connection is configured, it sends your email address and affirmative newsletter consent to a server endpoint for storage in Railway-hosted PostgreSQL. The record includes your email address, signup time, consent-text version, and signup source. If storage is unavailable, the form displays an error rather than confirming a signup.

The endpoint uses a keyed hash of your network address to limit repeated requests; it does not store the raw address in the newsletter tables. Rate-limit records are removed after they are more than one day old when subsequent requests are processed. Hosting providers may keep separate request logs under their own practices.

Signing up does not enroll you in text messages or unrelated services. The current integration stores signups; it does not send emails automatically or send a confirmation email. Contact emily@seattlathome.com to request removal or stop newsletter emails. Subscriber records remain until removed, subject to necessary business and legal obligations. Any future email delivery service and its unsubscribe process should be reflected here before use.

How information is used

Information you share may be used to respond to questions, schedule and prepare for meetings, provide requested real estate guidance, and maintain necessary business records. Technical information may be used to operate the site, diagnose problems, and protect against misuse. A website inquiry or booking is not, by itself, consent to unrelated promotional texts or emails.

When information may be shared

Information may be handled by service providers needed to deliver the service you request, such as scheduling, email, hosting, and business record systems. Where needed for a requested real estate service, information may also be shared with the brokerage or relevant transaction professionals. Other disclosures may be necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, or protect rights and safety. Additional disclosures and permissions may apply during an actual transaction.

Retention and security

Retention depends on the purpose of the information, the services used, and applicable brokerage, legal, and recordkeeping obligations. Local drafts remain in your browser until deleted or cleared. Third-party providers have their own retention practices. No fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule is promised by this policy.

No website, email system, or online service can guarantee complete security. If you need to share sensitive transaction records, ask Emily for an appropriate delivery method rather than using a general inquiry.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide optional information, contact Emily directly instead of booking online, and clear locally stored drafts. To ask about information you have provided, request a correction or deletion, or stop optional communications, use the contact details below. Requests are subject to applicable law, identity verification where appropriate, and required record retention. Your legal rights may vary by location.

Third-party policies

Third parties control their own websites and services. Review their notices when using them:

  • Calendly privacy notice
  • Vercel privacy policy
  • Railway privacy policy
  • Google and YouTube privacy policy
  • Meta privacy policy, including Instagram
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy
  • OpenFreeMap privacy policy

Children's information

This website is intended for adults seeking real estate information, not for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a related inquiry or booking, contact Emily so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This policy should be updated when the site's forms, hosting, analytics, integrations, or information practices change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.
