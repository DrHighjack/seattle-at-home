The process

Six steps, and I’m with you for each one.

  1. 01

    Consultation & representation

    We meet on video, talk through your goals, and put our working agreement in writing before we tour.

  2. 02

    Finances

    I’ll introduce you to lenders if you need one, and we’ll set a budget that feels comfortable, not just approved.

  3. 03

    Search

    I set up searches around your priorities, tour with you, and tell you what I notice about each home.

  4. 04

    Offer

    We look at recent sales together, decide on terms, and write an offer that’s competitive and still feels right to you.

  5. 05

    Due diligence

    Inspections, documents and deadlines. I keep track of all of it and explain what each finding means for you.

  6. 06

    Closing

    I coordinate with escrow, your lender and the other side, so closing day is about getting your keys.
