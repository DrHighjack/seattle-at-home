Selling with Emily

Questions Sellers Ask Me

Emily Hawkins | Seattle At Home
Emily@SeattleAtHome.com · 425-587-8838

Interviewing agents before you list is smart, and I encourage it. Here are the questions sellers ask me most, with my honest answers.

How long have you been in real estate, and how many homes have you sold?

I've been a broker since 2023 and a real estate investor since 2012, so I understand this market from an owner's point of view, not just an agent's. I've closed 40 transactions totaling $38.5M, including 13 listings, and 15 of those transactions closed in the last 12 months. All of my business comes from referrals, past clients, social media and my personal network.

I recently started my own independent business. It lets me be agile and flexible: I can meet you where you are and market your home the way I want to show up for you.

How quickly do your listings sell?

My listings sell fast. The median is 8 days on market, and every listing I've had has sold in under 30 days.

I also represent a lot of buyers, which helps you as a seller: I know exactly what makes buyers write their strongest offers, and I build every listing around that.

What is your commission?

My listing commission is between 3.5% and 4%, and it's always a conversation. Every home and every seller's goals are different, and I want this to work for you.

I'm a full-service broker, not a discount broker. You get my hands-on attention from prep to closing, plus my network of trusted vendors (stagers, cleaners, photographers, contractors and more) who prioritize my clients so things get done right and on time.

How will you market my home?

Your home gets the full marketing package:

  • Professional photography and video
  • A feature on my YouTube channel, where I do property tours
  • Social media promotion
  • Email marketing to my network
  • Direct outreach to local agents

I love open houses, and I'll host at least two the first weekend your home is live. Open houses build energy and momentum right out of the gate, and they put your home in front of buyers who might not have booked a private showing.

How do you decide on a list price?

Pricing comes from recent sold comps, and I look at your home the way an appraiser would while also factoring in current market trends. I focus on sales from the last 2–3 months, not 6. Rates, inventory and buyer behavior have shifted too much for older comps to tell the real story.

I also call the agents on comparable pending sales to find out what's really happening: how many offers came in, whether sellers are paying closing costs, and what kind of financing buyers are using. Having been in real estate as an investor since 2012, I also look at the numbers the way a buyer will, so your price holds up from day one.

Is there a best time of year to sell?

Honestly, there are times of year when it's more profitable to sell, and the data on that is very clear. If you have flexibility on timing, I'll help you line up your listing with the strongest window of the year.

Not every seller has that flexibility, and that's okay. Whenever you list, your home gets the same attention to pricing, preparation and presentation. December or April, every listing gets my full effort.

Will I work with you directly, and how will you keep me updated?

You'll work directly with me from our first conversation to closing day. Behind the scenes, my transaction coordinator and assistant keep the paperwork accurate and the timeline on track, and my vendor network gets things done quickly.

You'll get regular texts from me with updates, plus a weekly report on showings and buyer feedback. If something urgent comes up, I'll call you right away. You'll never have to wonder what's happening with your home.

A little about me

I started investing in real estate in 2012, and building passive income through property changed my life. That's what drives me as an agent: I want to help others use real estate as a tool to build wealth, whether that means selling for top dollar, buying a first home or growing a portfolio.

I'm a full-time agent. I research the market every day and stay ahead of the trends, so my advice is based on what's happening now, not six months ago. I'm always learning, and I bring that to every client and every sale.
