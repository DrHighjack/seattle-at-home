About Emily

Thoughtful guidance. Your life in mind.

I’m Emily Hawkins, a Seattle-area real estate broker. I help people make thoughtful, strategic decisions about their homes—and how those decisions fit into the life they’re building.

Buying, selling, renting, holding, downsizing, or managing a home from a distance: the starting point is what makes sense for you, financially and personally.

Buying or selling a home is a big deal. My job is to make it feel manageable, be honest about the choices, and stay in your corner after moving day.
