Questions people ask me

You might be wondering…

“Why start with a video call?”

Emily

It’s the easiest way to get to know each other, and you can join from your couch. In 45 minutes I can understand where you are and give you a plan you can use.

“What if I’m a year or more away from moving?”

Emily

That’s a great time to talk. Early conversations are where I can help the most, and we’ll work on your timeline, not mine.

“Will you pressure me to decide quickly?”

Emily

No. My job is to give you good information and my honest opinion. The decision, and the pace, are yours.

“What’s it like to text with you?”

Emily

Short questions get short answers, fast. Bigger questions get a call or a quick video, so nothing gets lost.
